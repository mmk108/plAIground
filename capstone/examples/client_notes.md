# Accounts payable team: how we work today (fictional example)

- Every week, collect new invoices from the shared inbox and log them in the spreadsheet
- Check each invoice amount against the purchase order, then flag mismatches
- Send a payment to the vendor once an invoice is approved
- Summarize monthly spend by vendor from the ERP export and send the report to the finance lead
- Negotiate payment terms with strategic suppliers
- Answer vendor emails asking about payment status
- Update the vendor master data when a supplier sends a new bank account
